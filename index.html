FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the demo top-up process.

How do I find my UID? +

Your UID is the unique player ID associated with your game account. Enter the UID exactly as shown in your account.

How does UID verification work? +

Enter your UID and select VERIFY UID. The demo connects to the configured player-information API and displays the returned player details when available.

How do I select a diamond package? +

After verification, continue to the store and choose the package you want. Select BUY to review the order before payment.

How does the payment page work? +

The payment page displays the selected package, amount, UID, order ID and the configured demo payment information.
